AE 582: Satellite Engineering

Aerospace Engineering Department

Instructor: Dr. Juan Carlos Martínez Oliveros

Course Catalog Description

The course will cover the main aspects of a space mission lifecycle from requirements development to spacecraft and ground system design, assembly integration and test, and in-orbit operations . It also includes mission statement and definition, Project organization, Mission design, System Engineering, Science & instruments . Multiple subsystems, including but not limited to Telecom, Electrical power, Structure and Configuration, C&DH, ADCS, Propulsion, Thermal .

Course Objectives

  • The main objective is to learn to apply the fundamentals of space system engineering & design .
  • The course introduces the various phases, systems, & subsystems involved in the design of spacecraft describing the techniques & best practices to plan .
  • Show a common understanding of all spacecraft fundamentals .

Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

  • CLO1. Understand the function of spacecraft subsystems .
  • CLO2. Understand the effect of launch system on satellite and payload design and performance .
  • CLO3. Apply orbital mechanics formula and tools to spacecraft mission design .
  • CLO4. Evaluate spacecraft subsystem performance and trades .

Learning Resources

  • SPACECRAFT SYSTEMS ENGINEERING Fourth Edition, Edited by Peter Fortescue, University of Southampton, UK Graham Swinerd, University of Southampton, UK, John Stark, Queen Mary, University of London, UK .
  • Space Mission Analysis and Design by James R. Wertz (Author), Wiley J. Larson (Author) .
