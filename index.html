Course Catalog Description

The course will cover the main aspects of a space mission lifecycle from requirements development to spacecraft and ground system design, assembly integration and test, and in-orbit operations[cite: 1]. It also includes mission statement and definition, Project organization, Mission design, System Engineering, Science & instruments[cite: 1]. Multiple subsystems, including but not limited to Telecom, Electrical power, Structure and Configuration, C&DH, ADCS, Propulsion, Thermal[cite: 1].

Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

  • CLO1. Understand the function of spacecraft subsystems[cite: 1].
  • CLO2. Understand the effect of launch system on satellite and payload design and performance[cite: 1].
  • CLO3. Apply orbital mechanics formula and tools to spacecraft mission design[cite: 1].
  • CLO4. Evaluate spacecraft subsystem performance and trades[cite: 1].
